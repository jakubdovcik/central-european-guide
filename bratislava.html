Bratislava_

  • Slovak capital on the Danube; 1 h to Vienna, 2 h to Budapest.
  • Old town: 15 min end to end on foot. A long weekend is plenty.
  • Currency: euro.

Bratislava Castle map

  • White four-towered "upturned table" on the hill; rebuilt 1950s–60s.
  • Grounds and baroque garden are free; best Danube view.
  • tip: come at sunset, walk down the old steps into town.

St. Martin's Cathedral map

  • Coronation church of Hungarian kings, 1563–1830 (incl. Maria Theresa).
  • Gilded crown on the 85 m spire; brass crowns in the pavement trace the route.

Michael's Gate & the lanes map

  • Last medieval gate; tower view + small weapons museum.
  • Kilometre-zero plaque under the arch.
  • Michalská and Ventúrska: the café and bar strip.

Main Square & Primate's Palace map

  • Old Town Hall (climb the tower), Roland fountain (1572).
  • Primate's Palace: Hall of Mirrors, where the Peace of Pressburg was signed (1805).
  • 17th-c. English tapestries found hidden in its walls.

UFO Bridge deck map

  • Flying saucer 95 m above the Danube (1972); lift, deck, restaurant.
  • Views to Austria and Hungary. Go just before sunset.

Blue Church map

  • All-blue Art Nouveau church by Ödön Lechner (1907–13).
  • Inside usually open only around mass; the outside is the point.

Slavín map

  • WWII Soviet memorial among embassy villas; ~6,800 soldiers buried.
  • Quiet, wide city view. 20–30 min uphill walk.

Čumil & bronze statues map

  • Čumil: worker peeking from a manhole (Panská x Rybárska brána).
  • Also: Schöne Náci tipping his hat, Napoleonic soldier on Main Square.

Hviezdoslav Square & Theatre map

  • Tree-lined promenade; 1886 National Theatre. Opera tickets cheap vs. Vienna.
  • Christmas markets late Nov–23 Dec: lokše, punch, mulled wine.

Danube riverside map

  • Embankment from the UFO bridge to Eurovea.
  • Cross Starý most to Sad Janka Kráľa (1770s), oldest public park in Central Europe.

Communist-era Bratislava

  • Slovak Radio: upside-down pyramid. map
  • Kamenné námestie: Hotel Kyjev, old Prior store.
  • Slovak National Gallery: bridge wing over a baroque barracks; free collection. map
  • Petržalka panel estate: best seen from the castle or the UFO.

Devín Castle map

  • Ruin on a cliff where the Morava meets the Danube.
  • Gate of Freedom: memorial to those killed crossing the Iron Curtain.
  • Bus 29 from under the UFO bridge, ~25 min.

Danubiana museum map

  • Modern art on a Danube peninsula near Čunovo, 15 km south.
  • Car, bike along the dam, or seasonal boat.

Small Carpathian wine map

  • Rača (tram), Svätý Jur, Pezinok, Modra: 20–40 min.
  • Try Frankovka, Veltlínske zelené, Riesling.
  • Open-cellar days around St. Martin's (Nov) and in spring.

Getting around

  • Vienna airport: ~50 min by direct bus. Bratislava airport: bus 61.
  • Vienna: hourly trains (~1 h); Twin City Liner boat in summer.
  • Trams/buses: IDS BK app, or bank card on board.
  • Taxis: Bolt, Uber or Hopin. Never hail on the street.
