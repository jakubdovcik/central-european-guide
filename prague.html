Prague_

  • About 4 hours by train from Bratislava or Vienna. Give it three days.
  • The Old Town is where most visitors stay, and it's the weakest part. Overpriced food, exchange booths, people handing out flyers. See it early in the morning and move on.
  • Spend your time in Letná, Vinohrady and Malá Strana instead. They're beautiful old neighbourhoods, and that's also where the good places to eat are.
  • Currency is the Czech koruna (CZK), not the euro.

    Letná map

    • Up on the plateau above the river. Big park, apartment blocks from the early 1900s, lots of young families.
    • From the edge of the park you see every bridge in a row.
    • The beer garden at Letenský zámeček has plastic cups, cheap beer and the best sunset in town.
    • A giant metronome stands where the Stalin monument used to be.
    • Good for breakfast and coffee (Eska, moe, see section 7). Walk on into Holešovice afterwards.

    Vinohrady map

    • Wide streets of Art Nouveau houses, built for the well-off around 1900. My favourite part of Prague to just walk.
    • Best density of good restaurants, cafés and wine bars in the city.
    • Riegrovy sady park has a beer garden with a view of the castle at sunset.
    • Náměstí Míru with its church, and Jiřího z Poděbrad with Plečnik's church and a farmers' market.

    Malá Strana map

    • Baroque streets under the castle. Touristy along the main route, quiet one street off it.
    • St. Nicholas Church, the Lennon Wall, Kampa island by the river.
    • Wallenstein Garden is free and has peacocks.
    • Some good places to eat here too, if you avoid the ones with photo menus.

    Holešovice map

    • Old industrial district next to Letná, now galleries, cafés and bars.
    • DOX for contemporary art (there's a huge wooden airship on the roof).
    • The old slaughterhouse is now Holešovice market. Veletržní palác has the National Gallery's modern art.

    Prague Castle & St. Vitus map

    • A huge complex: St. Vitus Cathedral, the Old Royal Palace, Golden Lane.
    • The courtyards are free. You need a ticket for the buildings.
    • Changing of the guard at noon.

    Petřín map

    • Hill with orchards and gardens. The funicular takes a normal transit ticket.
    • The lookout tower (1891) is a small copy of the Eiffel Tower. 299 steps.
    • Walk from here along the ridge to the castle.

    Worth seeing once, but it's a bit of a scam zone. Don't eat on the squares, don't change money at the booths, and don't buy trdelník. Come before 9 when it's empty, then leave for Letná or Vinohrady.

    Charles Bridge map

    • Stone bridge from 1357 with 30 baroque statues.
    • Before 8 in the morning it's beautiful. By 10 you can't move.

    Old Town Square & the Astronomical Clock map

    • The clock is from 1410. On the hour, little apostles go past the windows. It takes about 45 seconds.
    • Týn Church with its two spires. The town hall tower has a good view.

    Jewish Quarter (Josefov) map

    • The Old-New Synagogue (13th century) is still in use.
    • The Old Jewish Cemetery and the Spanish Synagogue are the other two to see.
    • One Jewish Museum ticket covers most sites. Closed on Saturdays.

    Vyšehrad map

    • Old fortress on a cliff above the river, south of the centre. Few tourists.
    • The cemetery has Dvořák, Smetana and Mucha.
    • Metro C to Vyšehrad, or walk there along the river.

    Žižkov TV Tower map

    • 216 m concrete tower from the 1980s. There's a viewing deck.
    • David Černý's giant faceless babies crawl up the sides.

    Wenceslas Square map

    • Where the crowds gathered in 1989. The National Museum is at the top.
    • Jan Palach's memorial is in front of the museum.

    5 · Kutná Hora · day trip

    • Beautiful medieval town about an hour east of Prague by train. It's on UNESCO's list.
    • In the Middle Ages it was rich from silver. The Bohemian kings minted their coins at the Italian Court here.
    • St. Barbara's, a huge Gothic church built by the miners.
    • The Sedlec ossuary, a chapel decorated with the bones of tens of thousands of people. Strange, and very popular.
    • The game Kingdom Come: Deliverance II is set here. In the game the town is called Kuttenberg, and you can walk the real streets. There are fan routes and costumed tours.
    • tip: the main station is in Sedlec, next to the ossuary. See that first, then take the small local train or walk 30 minutes to the old town.

    6 · One day

        07:30 ----- 10:00 ----- 13:00 ----- 15:00 ----- 18:00 ----- 21:00
      |           |           |           |           |           |
   bridge      castle       lunch       letná       sunset    vinohrady
    1. Charles Bridge at 7:30, then the Old Town Square while it's still empty.
    2. Cross to Malá Strana and walk up to the castle.
    3. Lunch in Malá Strana, or Kuchyň by the castle.
    4. Down and across to Letná. Coffee, the park, Holešovice if you have time.
    5. Sunset at the Letná beer garden or Riegrovy sady. Dinner and drinks in Vinohrady.

    Getting around

    • From the airport: Airport Express bus to the main station, or bus 119 to the metro.
    • Tickets in the PID Lítačka app or from the yellow machines.
    • Tram 22 goes past the castle, Malá Strana, Národní and through Vinohrady. Good for sightseeing.
    • Taxis: order through Bolt, Uber or Liftago. Don't take one off the street.

    Money

    • Cards work almost everywhere.
    • Skip the "0% commission" exchange booths. Use a bank ATM.
    • If a card machine offers to charge you in euros, say no and pay in CZK.
