Prague_

  • Czech capital on the Vltava; ~4 h by train from Bratislava or Vienna.
  • Bigger than Bratislava: plan 3 days, use trams and metro.
  • Currency: Czech koruna (CZK), not euro.

Old Town Square & Astronomical Clock map

  • Orloj (1410): apostles parade on the hour, 9:00–23:00.
  • Týn Church's twin spires; Old Town Hall tower for the view.
  • tip: skip the square's restaurants; eat a few streets away.

Charles Bridge map

  • Stone bridge begun 1357; 30 baroque statues.
  • Go before 8:00 or late evening; packed by 10.
  • Climb the Old Town bridge tower for the classic photo.

Jewish Quarter (Josefov) map

  • Old-New Synagogue: oldest active synagogue in Europe (13th c.).
  • Old Jewish Cemetery, Spanish Synagogue.
  • One Jewish Museum ticket covers most sites. Closed Saturdays.

Prague Castle & St. Vitus map

  • Largest ancient castle complex in the world.
  • St. Vitus Cathedral, Golden Lane, Old Royal Palace.
  • Courtyards free; buildings need a ticket. Guard change at noon.

Malá Strana map

  • Baroque Lesser Town below the castle; St. Nicholas Church.
  • Lennon Wall, Kampa Island, Wallenstein Garden (free, peacocks).

Petřín Hill map

  • Funicular up (normal transit ticket works).
  • 1891 lookout tower, a mini Eiffel; 299 steps.
  • Mirror maze, rose garden, walk down to the castle.

Wenceslas Square map

  • Boulevard of the 1989 Velvet Revolution.
  • National Museum at the top; Jan Palach memorial in front.

Žižkov TV Tower map

  • 216 m brutalist rocket (1985–92), observation deck.
  • David Černý's giant faceless babies crawl up the sides.

Letná beer garden map

  • Plastic cups, cheap beer, best view of the bridges.
  • Giant metronome where Stalin's statue stood.

Letná

  • Leafy, local, above the river; park + beer garden.
  • Breakfast at Eska or moe (see below).

Vinohrady map

  • Art Nouveau streets, best restaurant density in the city.
  • Riegrovy sady: beer garden with sunset over the castle.
  • Náměstí Míru; Jiřího z Poděbrad: Plečnik's church, farmers' market.

Holešovice & DOX map

  • Ex-industrial district, now galleries and cafés.
  • DOX: contemporary art, with the Gulliver airship on the roof.
  • Holešovice market (old slaughterhouse); Veletržní palác for modern art.

Vyšehrad map

  • Fortress on a cliff over the Vltava; few tourists.
  • Cemetery with Dvořák, Smetana, Mucha.
  • Metro C to Vyšehrad, or walk along the river.

      |           |           |           |           |           |

  1. Charles Bridge early, before the crowds.
  2. Malá Strana → up to the castle and St. Vitus.
  3. Lunch: Kuchyň by the castle, or back over the river.
  4. Old Town Square (clock on the hour) → Josefov.
  5. Sunset beer at Letná; dinner and drinks in Vinohrady.

Getting around

  • Airport: Airport Express bus to the main station, or bus 119 to metro A.
  • Transit: PID Lítačka app or yellow machines; 30/90-min or 24 h tickets.
  • Tram 22 passes most sights: castle, Malá Strana, Národní, Vinohrady.
  • Taxis: Bolt, Uber or Liftago. Never hail on the street.

Money

  • Pay by card almost everywhere.
  • Avoid "0% commission" exchange booths; use bank ATMs.
  • When a card machine offers euros, choose CZK.
