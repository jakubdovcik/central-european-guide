    Getting between them

    • Bratislava to Vienna: a train every hour, about an hour.
    • Bratislava to Prague: direct trains, about 4 hours.
    • Flying in? Vienna airport is closer to Bratislava than most people think, 50 minutes by bus.
    • Slovakia pays in euros, Czechia in koruna (CZK).
